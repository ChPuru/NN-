Why the preprocessing is the whole demo. MNIST digits were size-normalised into a 20×20 box and then centred in a 28×28 field by their centre of mass. Feed a raw canvas scrape to a genuinely 98%-accurate model and it drops to roughly 25%: the model never saw a digit at that scale or offset, and it has no translation invariance to fall back on. mnistify() in app.js reproduces the original pipeline step for step.

Where a plain MLP stops. 97.5–98.2% on the test set is the ceiling here. A small convolutional net reaches 99.2%+, because a convolution knows a stroke means the same thing wherever it lands; this model has to learn that fact 784 separate times.
